### About me

I'm the **founder and executive chairman of [Let's Cook](https://letscook.co.in)**, a student-run tech
community, and a second-year B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at GLA University.

I like building things people actually use: a phone line farmers can call to book a mandi slot, an
internship search that removes scam posts before you see them, AI agents that tell you whether a job
offer is fake. Most of my projects start at hackathons. The good ones keep going after the demo.
